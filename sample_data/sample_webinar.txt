Webinar Title: Introduction to Large Language Models in Engineering
Speaker: Prof. Alan Turing

1. Overview:
Today's session focuses on how modern LLMs use self-attention mechanisms to process tokens in parallel.
We examined transformer encoder-decoder blocks and the impact of context window sizes.

2. Key Engineering Concepts:
- Attention heads compute relevance scores across input sequence embeddings.
- Quantization reduces floating-point precision to allow running models on resource-constrained edge hardware.
- Prompt engineering and few-shot conditioning can steer generation without fine-tuning weights.
